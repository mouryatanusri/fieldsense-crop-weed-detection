      How it works

      A transparent computer-vision pipeline — no black box, every step is explainable.

      1. 1

        Segment vegetation

        The Excess-Green index (2G − R − B) with an adaptive Otsu threshold separates plants from soil, shadows and stones.

      2. 2

        Find the crop rows

        Plant pixels are projected across every angle; the sharpest periodic pattern gives the row direction and spacing.

      3. 3

        Classify each plant

        Plants on a row line at the typical crop size are crops. Off-row or undersized plants are weeds.

      4. 4

        Plan the treatment

        Weed density is gridded into a heat map and spray plan, estimating herbicide saved by treating only affected cells.

      FieldSense · Flask + OpenCV + vanilla JS Indicative analysis — confirm with field scouting before treatment.
